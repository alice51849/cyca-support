Support & FAQ

Need help? We're happy to assist.

How do I track my period?

Tap to log the day your period starts and how your flow feels. Cyca instantly updates your calendar and predictions for your next period, fertile window, and ovulation.

How accurate are the predictions?

Cyca predicts using the cycle history you log, so accuracy improves as you track more cycles. Predictions are estimates for planning and wellness, and cycles can naturally vary. Cyca is not a contraceptive and does not provide medical advice.

What is the Daily Body Forecast?

It's a gentle daily reading — based on where you are in your cycle — of how your energy, mood, skin, and comfort may feel, with caring suggestions for the day. It's supportive guidance, not a diagnosis.

Is my data really private?

Yes. Everything is stored on your device. Cyca has no account and no cloud, includes no ads or third-party tracking, and works fully offline.

Which languages are supported?

Cyca is fully localized in English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, and Italian.

How do free and Pro work?

Cyca is free to track today and your period. A one-time lifetime Pro unlock adds future predictions, Personal Rhythm insights, the Smart Planner, full calendar history, and deeper personalization — paid once, yours forever, with no subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your device. You can clear entries in the app, or delete the app to remove locally stored data, subject to normal iOS behavior and any device backups you control.
