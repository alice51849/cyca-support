When is a separate period tracker worth it? Cyca vs the Health app, Clue and a paper diary

Short answer: Cyca suits you if you want your period, fertile window and ovulation estimates on one calendar with no account, no cloud and no tracking, and you’d rather the data stayed on your iPhone. If you’re already happy with Cycle Tracking in Apple’s Health app, or you just need a record to show your GP, you probably don’t need another app. And if you’re trying to avoid pregnancy, Cyca isn’t the tool for that: it is not a medical device or contraception, so talk to your GP.

This page lays out the realistic options for keeping track of your cycle in Australia, including the free ones that come with your phone or cost nothing at all. Each cell sticks to what the provider itself says publicly, with a link so you can check it.

All four are fine ways to keep a record. They mostly differ on two things: whether your entries sit with a company or only with you, and how much the tool does for you in between periods.

When Cyca makes sense

  • You don’t want a login attached to your cycle. There’s nothing to sign up for. Open it and log.
  • You’d rather nothing went to a server. The listing says no cloud and no tracking, and the app works offline.
  • You want a bit more than dates. The Daily Body Forecast reads where you are in your cycle, and reminders can give you a heads-up before your next period or fertile window.
  • You don’t want a subscription. Tracking is free; Pro is a one-off unlock.

When you don’t need it

  • The Health app already works for you. It’s on every iPhone, it predicts your period and fertile window, and with a compatible Apple Watch it can use wrist temperature data too. Adding Cyca on top just means logging twice.
  • You want the same data on several devices. Cyca has no cloud, so there’s no account-based sync. An app that stores data on its servers, or the Health app with iCloud, is the better fit.
  • You only need something to show your GP. A notebook with start dates, length, heaviness and symptoms over a couple of cycles does that job, going by healthdirect’s advice.
  • You want to avoid or plan a pregnancy medically. Cyca gives estimates, not contraception or a diagnosis. Your GP or a family planning clinic is the right first stop.

Sounds like your situation? Cyca keeps your cycle on your iPhone, with no account and no cloud.

Questions

Can I use the Health app and Cyca at the same time?

You can, but you’d be logging in two places. Most people are better off picking one.

What happens to my Cyca data if I lose my phone?

Because there’s no account and no cloud, the developer doesn’t hold a copy. Your data lives on that iPhone, and the developer has no way to restore it for you.

Is a paper diary really good enough?

For a record to take to an appointment, yes. healthdirect suggests noting when your period starts, how long it lasts, how heavy it is and when it stops. What paper won’t do is estimate your next period or remind you.
