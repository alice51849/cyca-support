What is Cyca?

Cyca is an iPhone period and cycle tracker. You log your period, flow, cramps, moods and notes, and Cyca estimates your next period, fertile window and ovulation day on a calendar that adjusts every time you log. A Daily Body Forecast describes energy, mood, skin and comfort for where you are in your cycle, and optional reminders come before your next period. Its predictions are estimates for wellness and planning.

Who is Cyca for?

People who want a calm, simple way to log their cycle and see what may be coming next, and who care that the data stays on their own phone. Cyca has no servers and no account, and works fully offline.

How does Cyca charge?

Logging today and your period is included. A one-time lifetime unlock adds future predictions, Personal Rhythm, the Smart Planner, full calendar history and deeper personalization. The listing says it is never a subscription. For the current price, check the App Store page, because prices differ by country.

How is Cyca different from other cycle trackers?

Based on what the listing describes: your data never leaves your device and there is no account; it works fully offline; it pairs the calendar with a Daily Body Forecast and a daily Care Plan; Personal Rhythm and the Smart Planner highlight your best and gentlest days; and it is a single purchase, not a subscription. Cyca is not a medical device and is not a form of contraception.
