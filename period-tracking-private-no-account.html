What to log

ItemWhy it helps
First day of your periodThe starting point for cycle length, and the most important input for predicting your next period.
FlowHeavy, medium or light. Over time you learn what is typical for you, so changes are easier to notice.
CrampsWhich days hurt more and how much, so you can plan ahead and describe it clearly at an appointment.
Mood and notesHow you feel before your period, sleep, skin and so on. Seen alongside your cycle, patterns are easier to spot.

You do not need to log everything every day. Just noting the day your period starts is enough to begin.

How long until you see a pattern

Cycle length differs from person to person, and it is common for it to vary by a few days from month to month. One or two entries rarely show your pattern; after several cycles your average length and usual range become clearer. If your cycle is irregular, a log is even more useful: a clear record helps when you talk to a doctor.

Reading the calendar predictions

  • Next period: estimated from your past cycle lengths. The more you log, the closer it usually gets to your rhythm.
  • Fertile window and ovulation day: also estimated ranges based on your cycle, not test results.
  • Predictions adjust: they are recalculated each time you log, so this month's estimate and next month's can differ.
To be clear: period, ovulation and fertile window dates from any app are estimates. Cyca provides wellness and planning estimates and is not a medical device or a form of contraception.

Where does your period data live? Check before you pick an app

Period dates, symptoms and moods are personal health data. Before choosing a period tracker, read the App Store listing or privacy policy and check:

  • Do you have to create an account or sign in to use it?
  • Is your data stored on your phone, or uploaded to a server?
  • Can you log when you have no internet connection?

The simplest option is one that needs no account and keeps your data on your phone. For a longer checklist, see How to choose a private period tracker.

FAQ

Do I need to log every day?

No. Logging the first day of each period is enough to calculate cycle length. Adding flow, cramps and mood when you have time helps you see your own patterns.

Are period tracker predictions accurate?

Predictions are estimates based on your past cycles, and they usually get closer to your rhythm the more you log. Cyca provides wellness and planning estimates and is not a medical device or a form of contraception.

Do I need an account to use Cyca?

No. According to its App Store listing, Cyca has no servers and no account. Your data stays on your iPhone and the app works fully offline.

Can I track an irregular cycle?

Yes, and a log can be especially useful then. If your periods change or become irregular, a clear record can help when you talk to a doctor.
